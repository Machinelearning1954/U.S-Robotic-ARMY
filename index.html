// THE SYSTEM

How HUNT Works

HUNT doesn't ask the sky where it is. It looks at the world and figures it out — visual-inertial navigation, no satellites required.

1

See

Onboard cameras stream the world in real time. Every tree, wall, and wreck becomes a landmark instead of an obstacle to your signal.

2

Track

Visual odometry tracks distinctive features frame-to-frame, fusing with the IMU to estimate motion — dead reckoning that actually stays alive.

3

Map

SLAM builds a live 3D map while localizing inside it. The drone knows where it is because it remembers where it's been.

4

Hunt

Path planning replans around obstacles in milliseconds, holding the mission line through canopy, canyon, or chaos. Waypoint acquired. Next.

Course Catalog

Four classes. Zero satellites. Bring sunscreen and a spare set of props.

Ditch the Satellite

Intro to GPS-denied flight. Learn why your nav solution drifts, how an IMU lies to you over time, and how camera-based odometry keeps you honest. Final exam: fly a figure-8 under the Ocean Drive awnings.

6 WEEKS · BEGINNER · SIM + FIELD
HUNT-201

Feature Hunter

Visual odometry deep dive. Track corners, edges, and textures at 60fps; learn what kills your features (glass, water, fog) and how sensor fusion saves the day. Field trip: the mirrored towers of the Financial District.

8 WEEKS · INTERMEDIATE · PREREQ 101
HUNT-301

Map or Be Mapped

SLAM in anger. Build live 3D maps of the Grassrivers swamp while localizing inside them, close loops before drift closes you, and replan paths around gators — uh, obstacles — in real time.

10 WEEKS · ADVANCED · PREREQ 201

Signal Not Found

The capstone. Full mission in a simulated post-hurricane blackout zone: jammed spectrum, dust-choked air, collapsed landmarks. Navigate, map, deliver, return. Pass this and Leonida's worst days need you.
