# The Tichfield Culinary Academy — "Fort Flavor"

> **FICTIONAL VIDEO GAME CONTENT.** Part of the PAUDC game world. The Academy is an
> invented location loosely inspired by the historic peninsula campus architecture of
> Port Antonio, fully fictionalized for entertainment (as open-world games do with
> real cities). It depicts no real school, staff, students, or institution. See
> `PAUDC_Base_Design.md` for the world bible and content boundary rules.

---

## 1. Concept

On the game's fictional Tichfield peninsula — a finger of land between the twin
harbors, below the old colonial fort walls — stands the **Tichfield Culinary
Academy**, an adult cooking school and community kitchen run jointly by the town and
PAUDC's Morale & Family Command. The soldiers call it **"Fort Flavor."**

By day it's yellow-and-white colonial-era buildings, sea breeze through open
shutters, and the sound of pots and laughter. By night the courtyard becomes a
lantern-lit cookout where the smell of curry goat drifts across the water to the
base — canonically the reason PAUDC's mess hall has an inferiority complex.

It is the game's **cooking gameplay hub**: where players learn recipes, run food
missions, and where the base's culinary storylines (Chef Marlin, the unlicensed
jerk-chicken stand, canteen-credit economy) all converge.

## 2. The Campus

- **The Old Hall** — a historic assembly building converted into the main teaching
  kitchen: twelve cooking stations under ceiling fans, a blackboard of the week's
  recipe, trophies from (fictional) national cook-off victories.
- **The Fort Kitchen** — an outdoor live-fire yard built against the old fort wall:
  drum grills, pimento-wood smoke pits, and a legendary cast-iron pot called
  **"Granny"** that only the head instructor may stir.
- **The Garden Terraces** — scotch bonnet, thyme, scallion, and callaloo beds
  stepping down toward the water; players harvest ingredients here.
- **The Harbor Pantry** — a small dock where fishermen NPCs land the morning catch;
  price haggling minigame.
- **The Exam Room** — a tasting parlor where the instructors judge final dishes,
  studio-lit because every exam is also filmed for OnWatch.

## 3. Faculty (Original NPCs)

- **Headmistress "Miss Birdie" Castell** — retired competition chef, runs the Academy
  like a drill sergeant runs a parade ground; she and Auntie Blades are old friends
  who publicly deny it. Signature line: *"Taste it AGAIN. Yuh rushing it."*
- **Instructor Delroy "Low Heat" Palmer** — unhurried master of curries and stews;
  his curry goat lesson is the Academy's crown jewel and the game's most soothing
  quest. Nothing he cooks can be rushed, including his dialogue.
- **Pastry Sergeant Winnie** — an active-duty PAUDC cook seconded to the Academy;
  bakes under pressure, speaks entirely in fire-control metaphors.

## 4. Cooking Gameplay

### The Recipe System
Players learn **24 fictionalized Jamaican-inspired recipes** across four tracks —
Curries & Stews (curry goat is the capstone), Grill & Smoke, Sea Kitchen, and
Sweets & Festival. Cooking is a hands-on minigame: prep rhythm taps, seasoning
ratios by ear ("listen to di pot"), heat management on live fire, and a plating
flourish. Quality tiers: *Eatable → Nice → Big Pot → Granny-Approved.*

### The Curry Goat Questline — "Low and Slow"
A five-part story quest with Instructor Palmer: source the meat from a hill farmer
(jeep run through the switchbacks), trade for scotch bonnets at the market, learn
the burnt-curry recovery trick, defend the simmering pot from a crocodile with
opinions, and serve the final pot at a Palm Line Friday cookout. Completing it
unlocks curry goat as a craftable **squad buff meal** — the game's best stat food.

### Cooking as an Economy Loop
- Dishes sell for canteen credits at Palm Line, or cash at the town market.
- **Meal buffs**: stamina, swim speed, storm resistance (pepper pot), YARDCLASH
  hype-meter gain (festival dumplings).
- Chef Marlin's gray-market stand buys "Granny-Approved" dishes at a premium —
  putting the player between him and Miss Birdie's fierce disapproval, a running
  side-story.

### Events
- **Friday Fireside** — cook-off before YARDCLASH night; winner's dish becomes the
  arena's crowd snack, adding their buff to the whole venue.
- **Storm Kitchen** — during SC-2 and worse, the Academy becomes a community
  kitchen; players run cooking relays to feed shelters for major Standing and clout.
- **The Peninsula Plate** — seasonal tournament judged by Miss Birdie, the base
  commander, and one randomly selected NPC with chaotic taste.

## 5. Why It Fits PAUDC

The Academy is the soft-power heart of the game world: the place where base and town
actually mix. Recruitment lore says more soldiers enlisted "because of the food at
Fort Flavor" than because of any Skyfence demo — and the game quietly makes that
true: feeding NPCs raises the faction's recruitment world-stat just like viral
OnWatch clips do.
