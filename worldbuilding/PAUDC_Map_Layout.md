## 1. Spatial Overview — Reef to Peak

PAUDC climbs four biome bands from north (sea) to south (summit). Read the map
bottom of the water to top of the ridge and you read the player's career arc:

```
      OPEN SEA / REEF SHALLOWS          — ferry lane, Duppy Cay islet
  ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │  RING I  · SHORELINE   Z7 Palm Line ── Z4 Ironbeach ── Z5 The Strip
  │                         (west coast)    (twin-bay piers)   (east coast runway)
  │                         └────── A1 Seawall Highway ──────┘
  ├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │  RING II · GARRISON    Z6 The Crucible (jungle valley, center-west)
  │                         Machete Switchbacks climb from Palm Line
  ├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │  RING III· HIGHLANDS   Z2 Skyfence (hex gardens, mid-slope east)
  │                         Z3 The Ear (radome plateau, upper east)
  │                         Mist Line cable car crosses this whole band
  ├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │  RING IV · CITADEL     Z1 The Vault (inside Mount Nonsuch ridge)
  │                         reached by Vault Approach road + Geo-Core Tunnel
  └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**How the seven zones connect:**

- **Palm Line (Z7)**, **Ironbeach (Z4)** and **The Strip (Z5)** are strung along the
  **A1 Seawall Highway** — one continuous coastal drive, west to east. This is the
  public-facing face of the base and the busiest traffic band.
- **The Crucible (Z6)** sits one band inland. The **Machete Switchbacks** leave the
  highway beside Palm Line and zigzag up through the training valley; every recruit's
  first drive is this road, in the rain, in a governed jeep.
- **Skyfence (Z2)** continues off the switchbacks' eastern leg, mid-slope, where the
  hex launch gardens terrace the hillside. Reload convoys run highway → switchbacks →
  Skyfence during defense events.
- **The Ear (Z3)** has no public road. It is reached by the **Mist Line cable car**
  (lower station above Palm Line, four towers, upper station on the radome plateau)
  or by a restricted service track from Skyfence.
- **The Vault (Z1)** has exactly two ways in: the **Vault Approach** road from
  Skyfence ending at the 40-ton blast door, and the **Geo-Core Tunnel** bored from
  The Ear's plateau directly into the citadel — the "commuter" route for cleared
  signals staff, and the setting of the *Numbers Station* mission finale.
- **Water side:** Ironbeach's twin piers anchor the **coastal ferry**, the jet-ski
  spawn, and the *Maroon Thunder*'s berth. The Strip's runway overrun points
  straight out over the reef, so air and sea traffic cross at the map's northeast
  corner — deliberately, for spectacle.

## 2. Travel Routes

| Route | Path | Character |
|---|---|---|
| **A1 Seawall Highway** | Palm Line ↔ Ironbeach ↔ The Strip (full coast) | Fast, flat, scenic; leaderboard billboard visible at the Crucible turnoff; NPC traffic, checkpoint at each ring gate |
| **Machete Switchbacks** | Palm Line ↔ The Crucible ↔ Skyfence | Tight hairpins, mud physics in storms, convoy-escort event route; barrier rails are cosmetic — respect the drop |
| **Mist Line Cable Car** | Palm Line upper station ↔ The Ear plateau | 4 towers, glass gondolas, auto-ride with free camera; crosses above the Crucible and Skyfence — best recon of the whole base |
| **Coastal Ferry** | West landing ↔ Ironbeach piers ↔ East landing (fast travel) | Menu fast-travel in fiction; skippable cutscene of the bay at night |
| **Vault Approach** | Skyfence ↔ Blast Door (Ring IV gate) | Short, steep, floodlit; scripted door-opening set piece on first entry |
| **Geo-Core Tunnel** | The Ear ↔ The Vault interior | Rank-gated tram tunnel; teal service lighting, sub-bass Geo-Core hum grows as you approach |

## 4. The Four Access Rings

Rank gates are physical: gates, fences, MP checkpoints, and scanner arches that
turn from red to teal as you rank up. Un-cleared players who slip past accrue
**Watch Level** instead of wanted stars.

| Ring | Band | Zones | Opens At (fictional faction rank) | What Unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Ring I — Shoreline** | Coast | Palm Line, Ironbeach public piers, Strip viewing berm | **Visitor / Recruit** (anyone; enlistment at the Recruitment Pavilion) | Social hub, minigames, ferry, jet-ski rentals, Yardclash spectating |
| **Ring II — Garrison** | Lower valley | The Crucible, Ironbeach working piers, Strip aprons | **Private (E-1)** — finish "Open House" | Training trials, boat/aircraft passenger seats, barracks bunk, Yard ladder |
| **Ring III — Highlands** | Mid/upper slopes | Skyfence, The Ear, cable car upper station | **Specialist (E-4)** — pick a specialization track | Skyfence consoles, KINGFISHER intel upgrades, DFS side missions, solo flight certs |
| **Ring IV — Citadel** | Ridge interior | The Vault, Geo-Core Tunnel, ORACLE floor | **Officer (O-1) story clearance; full access at Command Rank** | Story briefings, player office, base-defense event authority |

## 5. Streaming-Ring Design Notes

The four access rings double as the world-streaming architecture — the fiction and
the tech budget are the same drawing:

- **Ring gates are airlocks for content.** Every checkpoint sits on a natural
  chokepoint (hairpin, tunnel mouth, cable car station) so the engine gets 8–20
  seconds of predictable player path to swap high-detail sets in and out. No loading
  screens; the MP waving you through *is* the loading screen.
- **One ring resident, neighbors low-LOD.** Only the ring the player occupies streams
  full interiors and crowd density. Adjacent rings hold silhouette geometry, lit
  windows, and audio beds — the Geo-Core glow and radome silhouettes are baked
  impostors until Ring III/IV actually load.
- **The cable car is a streaming corridor.** The Mist Line's fixed 2:30 ride crosses
  two ring boundaries; its scripted mist pockets are guaranteed occlusion windows for
  the Ring III load. Same trick, vertically, for the Geo-Core Tunnel tram.
- **Vertical seam, not radial.** Because rings stack by elevation, the streaming
  boundary is mostly a contour line — cheap to test against (altitude + region mask)
  and it never bisects a firefight arena.
- **Storm states pre-warm.** When the Storm Condition ladder ticks toward SC-2, the
  streamer pre-loads storm geometry variants ring by ring, starting from the coast —
  which is also the in-fiction order the base boards its shutters.
- **Rank = residency.** A player's rank caps which rings can be their spawn/apartment
  ring, which keeps hub-density load predictable per player cohort in shared sessions.

## 6. Landmarks & Photo Spots

1. **The Blooming Field (Skyfence)** — catch a test bloom at dusk when all nine hex
   petals open at once; the game's most-shared OnWatch screenshot.
2. **Geo-Core Vents (Mount Nonsuch ridgeline)** — three teal plumes visible from the
   whole map; stand between them for the "powered by the mountain" hero shot.
3. **Sunrise Deck (The Ear)** — the railed walkway between the two big radomes;
   canonical best sunrise on the island, per the DFS analysts who never leave.
4. **Reef Threshold (The Strip)** — the runway overrun over clear reef water; time
   your photo to a landing SV-77 and the rays gliding under the glide path.
5. **The Maroon Thunder's Well Deck (Ironbeach)** — drive a boat straight into the
   neon-lit carrier and photograph the wake lines from inside the ship.
6. **Lagoon Club Seawall (Palm Line)** — Friday night sound-system dances with the
   open-air cinema glowing behind; the social-hub postcard.
7. **Duppy Cay** — the tiny islet in the bay; swim out at night for the full base
   panorama, neon perimeter mirrored in the water.
8. **The Leaderboard Billboard (Crucible turnoff, A1)** — your name in lights over
   the highway if you top the Machete Course; obligatory rank-up selfie spot.
9. **Mist Line Tower 3** — climbable cable car pylon above the Crucible; watch a
   whole squad wargame play out below you.
10. **The Blast Door (Vault Approach)** — the 40-ton door mid-grind is a timed photo
    challenge; MPs will pose with you if your Standing is high enough.
