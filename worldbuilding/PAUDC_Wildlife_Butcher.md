# Wildlife & The Blue Mountain Butcher — Hunting, Processing & Trade

> **FICTIONAL VIDEO GAME CONTENT.** Part of the PAUDC game world. All mechanics are
> stylized, cartoon-abstracted game systems (GTA Online-style crafting) — no real-world
> processing instructions, no graphic content. See `PAUDC_Base_Design.md` for the
> world bible and content boundary rules.

---

## 1. The Wildlife Ecosystem

The island's interior is alive: goats on every hillside road (traffic hazard and
running joke), wild hogs in the banana groves, red snapper and kingfish on the reef,
and — because this is a GTA-style world — an **escaped exotic menagerie**. Lore: a
bankrupt billionaire collector's "private eco-park" up in the hills lost its fences in
a hurricane years ago. Now komodo dragons sun themselves on the coastal highway,
an anaconda lives somewhere in the Grassrivers-style wetland, and fishermen swear
something enormous cruises the deep trench offshore.

- **Common:** goats, hogs, chickens, snapper, crabs.
- **Uncommon:** wild boar "Hill Bosses," lionfish (invasive — the town pays bounties),
  kingfish.
- **Rare:** komodo dragons (each has a name and a wanted-poster at the market).
- **Exotic:** "Big Mumma," the wetland anaconda — a repeatable boss encounter.
- **Legendary:** **the Trench Shadow** — a megalodon-sized mystery fish. Never
  caught, only "witnessed." Deep-sea questline; the reward is a **Megalodon Tooth**,
  a legendary artifact, not meat.

Capture is stylized: tranq darts, nets, fishing rods, and for the big stuff, winch
rigs borrowed from the Mudfish engineers. Downed animals convert to a **capture
icon** in the player's cargo — no carcass rendering.

## 2. Blue Mountain Butcher & Market

A zinc-roofed market compound on the road between Palm Line and the Crucible,
run by **Miss Ouida**, a butcher-shop matriarch with a gold tooth and a ledger she
calls "the Bible." Half legitimate market, half information exchange — everyone
from Chef Marlin to DFS analysts passes through.

### The Processing Bench (stylized)
Pure cartoon crafting, zero gore:

1. Player places the **captured animal icon** on the bench.
2. **"The Contraption"** — a Rube Goldberg machine of gears, chutes, bright lights,
   steam whistles, and a bicycle-powered flywheel — rattles through a comedic
   animation (think claw machine meets carnival scale).
3. A wrapped, labeled product pops out the far end:
   - **Goat Meat Pack**
   - **Boar Meat Pack**
   - **Komodo Hide**
   - **Anaconda Oil**
   - **Red Snapper Fillet**
   - **Megalodon Tooth** *(Legendary Item — display trophy, never sellable twice)*

### Butcher Skill Tree — Levels 1–10
Stylized mastery, better business, no realism:

| Level | Perk |
|---|---|
| 1-2 | Basic processing; common animals only |
| 3-4 | +10% sell prices; Contraption runs faster |
| 5 | Uncommon/rare animals accepted; unlock delivery van |
| 6-7 | +25% prices; double-output crit chance ("Clean Pull!") |
| 8 | Exotic animals accepted; Miss Ouida shares black-market rumors |
| 9 | +40% prices; bulk orders from the PAUDC mess hall |
| 10 | "Master of the Mountain" — legendary quest access, custom shop livery |

### Hygiene Mini-Game
Comedic shop-keeping between processing runs: wipe counters with big cartoon
sparkle effects, sanitize tools in a glowing dunk tank, shoo a persistent stray dog
named **Inspector**. Keeps the **Shop Rating** (1-5 stars) high — higher rating means
better walk-in prices and a weekly bonus from the (fictional) Parish Health Officer,
who arrives unannounced like a boss encounter with a clipboard.

## 3. Selling & Dynamic Pricing

### Buyers
- **Local restaurants** — steady demand, best for common goods.
- **PAUDC mess hall** — bulk contracts, paid in canteen credits; the mess sergeant
  is desperate to compete with Fort Flavor.
- **Street vendors** — small lots, instant cash, prices swing with foot traffic.
- **Black-market exotic buyers** *(fictional)* — Barometer Syndicate middlemen who
  pay triple for rare goods but raise the player's profile with the villains; sell too
  often and Syndicate "repeat customers" start following you home. Risk/reward lever.

### Price Tiers
| Good | Tier | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Goat Meat Pack | Common | Price dips when the hills are "overgrazed" (overhunting debuff) |
| Red Snapper Fillet | Medium | Spikes during Friday Fireside and festival events |
| Komodo Hide | Rare | Restaurants won't touch it; Syndicate loves it |
| Anaconda Oil | Exotic | Sistren Ivy claims it's a YARDCLASH training rub; demand follows fight nights |
| Megalodon Tooth | Legendary | One-time artifact sale — or keep it mounted in your apartment for a permanent clout aura |

### Ecosystem Guardrail (game balance + tone)
Overhunt any species and it visibly thins out — prices crash, Miss Ouida refuses
stock ("Yuh mad? Let di hills breathe."), and a **Rewilding side mission** chain opens
to restore it. Keeps the loop sustainable and the humor intact.

## 4. Crocodiles & Alligators — The Apex Expansion

Two species, two completely different game loops — and a built-in tone rule:

### Native Crocodiles — protected, never processed
The crocodile is the island's national symbol (it's on the fictional parish crest in
the game, as in life), so in-game **native crocs are protected**: they can't be hunted,
butchered, or sold. Instead they're the stars of the **Croc Wrangler** loop:

- **Wrangler Contracts:** a croc in a swimming pool, a croc under a parked Duppy
  tilt-jet, a croc asleep in the YARDCLASH ring an hour before fight night. Player
  gets a snare pole, a tow skiff, and a timer — relocate it to the wetland preserve
  without getting spun. Paid by the parish in cash *and* Standing.
- **"Barrister"** — the canon celebrity: the enormous, unbothered croc who keeps
  breaking into the Pressure Dome (and once guarded Instructor Palmer's curry pot).
  He cannot be relocated permanently; he always comes back. Recurring comedy
  engine, OnWatch superstar, unofficial base mascot with his own merch cosmetics.
- **Croc-jacking (the danger mechanic):** wetland and river-mouth swimming is now
  genuinely risky — a lurking silhouette, a music sting, and a QTE dive-escape. Death
  by croc gets a special respawn quip. Airboats and elevated walkways become the
  smart traversal choice through Grassrivers-style swamp.

### Invasive Alligators — the menagerie strikes again
The escaped eco-park had a gator lagoon. Gators are **invasive**, so the parish pays
bounties and the full capture-and-process economy applies:

| Product | Tier | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Gator Hide | Rare | The Syndicate pays premium; Miss Ouida makes YARDCLASH champion belts from it |
| Gator Meat Pack | Medium | Fort Flavor unlocks a stewed gator recipe at Butcher Level 6 |
| "Swamp Gold" Teeth | Rare | Crafting component for the apartment trophy wall |

- **Nest Events:** gator nests spawn near fishing spots and crash local prices until
  cleared — a dynamic mini-economy disruption with a visible map icon.
- **"The General":** a legendary bull gator boss in the deep swamp — night-only,
  storm-only (SC-2 or worse), the land counterpart to the Trench Shadow questline.

### Telling them apart (a game mechanic, not a lecture)
New players WILL snare-pole a protected croc by mistake. First offense: Miss Ouida
confiscates it, one-star Shop Rating hit, and a mandatory 90-second "Know Yuh
Reptile" educational filmstrip in the market's back room — played completely
straight, instantly skippable never, and one of the game's best jokes. Repeat
offenses escalate Watch Level. The parish scanner app (a free OnWatch filter)
overlays a green/red outline on any basking reptile, turning identification into a
tiny skill players actually learn.

## 6. Hooks Into Existing Systems

- **Culinary Academy:** Granny-Approved dishes made with fresh Butcher & Market
  ingredients get a quality-tier head start — hunters and cooks feed one economy.
- **Storm Conditions:** SC-2 and worse closes the fishing grounds and sends exotic
  animals wandering into town — emergency capture missions, big payouts.
- **OnWatch:** capturing a rare animal on camera is top-tier clout; the komodo
  wanted-posters are community events with leaderboards.
- **Story:** Miss Ouida's ledger becomes evidence in the Barometer Syndicate
  campaign arc — the black-market buyers were never just buying meat.
- **Crocs & gators:** Wrangler Contracts pay Standing (discipline currency), gator
  hides feed YARDCLASH championship cosmetics, and Barrister's Pressure Dome
  break-ins periodically shut down fight night — a world-event everyone films.
